Question: What are the truly free apps for managing personal tasks and notes, like Evernote and Obsidian, that store your data in the cloud so you can reach it from any device?

What this means for Bojji

  • Nothing does notes plus tasks, open source, with real free cloud sync and no strings. Notesnook comes closest but caps attachments and doesn't do tasks. Anytype caps file storage.
  • Most of the free options make you bring your own storage. Joplin and Obsidian are free if you hook up Dropbox, Drive or Git yourself. That works, but it's fiddly, especially on mobile.
  • Free sync costs someone money. That's why every hosted free tier has a cap. To stay truly free, Bojji could store your data in cloud storage you already have (Google Drive, Dropbox, GitHub or S3) instead of running its own servers. The goal would be to make that setup painless, which is where Joplin and Obsidian fall short.
